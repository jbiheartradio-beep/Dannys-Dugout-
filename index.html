Meet Coach Danny

I am an experienced slowpitch softball player in the Tampa Bay area with a deep-rooted passion for the game that spans decades. My journey is built on a foundation of mastering fundamentals and a commitment to growing the game at every level.

I founded the South Tampa BP Club to provide a community for local players to get consistent, high-quality reps. Today, through Danny’s Dugout, I provide specialized instruction for adult athletes looking to dominate their local leagues and reach their peak performance.

Yes! You are welcome to split a session with a teammate. This is highly recommended for advanced players to keep reps fresh and prevent fatigue.

What should I bring? ▼

Bring your glove. I provide the balls and high-end bats for all sessions.

Where do lessons happen? ▼

We coordinate parks in Tampa, Clearwater, or St. Pete based on your location.
