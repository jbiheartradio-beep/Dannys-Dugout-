Meet Coach Danny

I am an experienced slowpitch softball player serving the Tampa, Clearwater, and St. Pete area with a deep-rooted passion for the game. My coaching is built on mastering fundamentals and a commitment to growing the game.

I founded the South Tampa BP Club to provide a community for local players. Through Danny’s Dugout, I provide specialized instruction for adult athletes looking to dominate their local leagues.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I bring a teammate? ▼

Yes! You can split any session with a teammate. Sharing reps is a great way for players to get high-intensity practice without burning out.

What should I bring? ▼

Just bring your glove. I provide professional-grade softballs and a variety of high-end bats for use during all sessions.

Where are the lessons held? ▼

We coordinate at local parks throughout Tampa, Clearwater, and St. Pete. Once you book, we'll confirm the best field based on your location.
